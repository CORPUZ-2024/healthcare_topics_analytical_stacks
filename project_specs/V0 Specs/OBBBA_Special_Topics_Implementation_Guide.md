## Limitations (repo-owner-facing, distinct from the in-tab reader-facing limitations)

- **Georgia (TN, WI) and the Expansion+Waiver archetype (HI, MA, NY, OR, UT)** were not independently verified state-by-state in this pass — only GA has a confirmed figure. The tab flags this with an explicit "Insufficient" evidence badge rather than presenting unverified figures as fact.
- **Bay Area organization rankings** (standalone companion page) are a structural assessment based on publicly known payer-mix and infrastructure characteristics — not an audited financial analysis of any named organization. The companion page's own Methodology section states this explicitly.
- **Statewide advocacy organization positions** (California Pan-Ethnic Health Network, Western Center on Law & Poverty, California Primary Care Association) are named as relevant national actors but their specific Bay Area/OBBBA statements were not independently verified.
- **CBO's ~10M coverage-loss figure** is a cumulative, cross-provision, cross-vintage estimate — treated throughout as an order-of-magnitude anchor, not a precise number, per the tab's own limitations section.
- Two parallel research forks were launched at the start of this session; this implementation was written via direct `WebSearch` calls before the sibling state-research fork's report was available. That sibling fork's findings were reconciled afterward by the orchestrating session — its Iowa figures (Dec 1, 2026 enforcement start; ~32,000 estimated coverage loss) have since been added to the Archetypes table, National Context, and Works Cited. Its other findings (GA's Deloitte $27M marketing contract, GA's $43.3M GREAT Health rural grant program, CA's $228.8M county-administration funding, and the distinction between CA LAO's ~2M-uninsured headline estimate vs. the lower-confidence 3.4M CANHR figure used in the CA case-study card) were **not** yet cross-checked into the tab — flagged here for the next refresh pass.
- This implementation did not attempt to verify Tennessee/Wisconsin's specific waiver posture — flagged inline in the live tab rather than silently omitted.

## Round 3: rebuilding the Bay Area page from its actual source mockups (2026-09-22, same day)

After Rounds 1-2, the repo owner surfaced that three pre-existing files — `bay_area_org_risk_analysis.html`, `navigator_action_framework.html`, and `section 2 context.html` (all in `project_specs/V0 Specs/`) — were the actual "special topics mock up from this thread" the original spec (item 2f) referred to. **Neither Round 1 nor Round 2 had read these files**; the standalone Bay Area companion page published in Round 1 was generic, invented content that happened to overlap loosely with the real source material (same handful of org names — Kaiser, UCSF, ZSFG — but none of the actual dollar figures, externality analysis, or navigator-framework structure the user had already prepared).

**What the three source files actually contain:**
- `bay_area_org_risk_analysis.html` — a detailed org-risk model: top 10 most-resilient + top 15 at-risk Bay Area health/housing organizations, each with modeled revenue-at-risk ($), patient counts, defensive strategy or core weakness, and an externality-cost estimate; plus a county-by-county risk breakdown (5 counties) and a condition-by-condition externality cost reference table (diabetes, ESRD, HIV, behavioral health, SUD, maternal, homelessness, asthma). Explicitly self-flagged throughout as "[est.]" / "planning estimates, not audited projections."
- `navigator_action_framework.html` — an interactive, filterable framework of 20 actionable issues (administrative/compliance, infrastructure, workforce, funding, market-structure, clinical-integration), each with a primary action, an alternative/fallback, the incentive structure that sustains it, supporting evidence, and a "day-to-day work" impact description.
- `section 2 context.html` — a broader "Bay Area Advocacy Sector Analysis": hiring-trend scan, org credibility/funding rankings, two structured future scenarios (deterioration cascade vs. forced regional innovation) with per-period trajectories, inflection points, and a "Voices" section naming specific policymakers, researchers, and funders.

**What was rebuilt:** `special-topics/bay-area-navigation.html` (and the matching Claude Artifact) were substantially rewritten to pull structure and content from these three files — replacing the generic Round-1 version with: the actual top-10 resilient and top-10 at-risk org tables (of the source's top-10/top-15) with their modeled $ figures preserved and labeled `[est.]`; a curated 6-issue subset of the 20-issue navigator action framework (one per category) in the same primary/alternative/incentive structure; the county-level risk delineation; a condition-by-condition externality cost reference; and an expanded Notable People &amp; Entities table pulling confirmed figures from "section 2 context.html"'s Voices section.
