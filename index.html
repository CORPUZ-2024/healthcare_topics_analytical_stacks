Coding System Distinctions

How the five core Medicare coding and payment classification systems relate to each other — what they do, where they apply, and how they interact in bundling and value-based care contexts.

Feature DRG
Diagnosis-Related Group		 HCPCS
Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System		 HCC
Hierarchical Condition Category		 ICD
International Classification of Diseases		 APC
Ambulatory Payment Classification
Primary use Inpatient hospital payment under IPPS. Billing codes for procedures, supplies, drugs, and services (FFS). Level I = CPT; Level II = non-physician supplies/drugs/etc. Risk adjustment to predict annual expected cost (premiums/capitation and quality models). Clinical classification of diagnoses (ICD-10-CM) and inpatient procedures (ICD-10-PCS). Hospital outpatient payment under OPPS.
Care setting Acute inpatient stays. All settings (professional, outpatient, ASC, DME, ambulance, etc.). Population-level risk for Medicare Advantage/ACA and some Medicaid models. All settings; foundational clinical coding on every claim/record. Hospital outpatient departments (HOPDs); some ED/clinic/ancillary.
Payment model Episode-based: one payment per stay, adjusted by DRG weight, wage index, etc. Fee-for-service: line-item payment per code (subject to edits, bundling). Risk-adjusted: additive risk score applied to premiums/benchmarks. Not a payment system by itself; feeds DRG, HCC, quality, epi studies. Prospective: APC weight × conversion factor; packaging & multiple-procedure rules under OPPS.
Basis of grouping Groups from ICD-10-CM/PCS diagnoses & procedures, age/sex/discharge status; CC/MCC tiers. CPT/HCPCS codes describing the specific service/supply/drug; units and modifiers refine payment. Mapped from ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes recorded over a look-back year; disease hierarchies. ICD-10-CM (diagnoses) and ICD-10-PCS (inpatient procedures) with clinical rules/edits. Groups CPT/HCPCS codes into clinical/resource bundles (status indicators determine packaging).
Scope Single inpatient episode. Individual services and supplies. Annual view of chronic disease burden per member. Complete clinical picture across encounters (conditions & procedures). An outpatient visit/encounter; multiple APCs may apply.
Additivity / interaction Hierarchical: one DRG per stay; CC/MCC levels modify payment (not additive). Generally not additive across bundled edits; each line evaluated (NCCI edits, modifiers). Additive with hierarchies: most unrelated HCCs add; within a disease family, higher-severity HCC suppresses lower. Multiple diagnoses/procedures can be listed; downstream systems (DRG/HCC) impose hierarchy. Mixed: some APCs pay separately; many ancillary items are packaged into a primary APC; multiples discounted.
Typical inputs Principal diagnosis, OR/non-OR procedures, comorbidities (CC/MCC), demographics, discharge status. Procedure code + units + modifiers; sometimes drug strength (J-codes), DME details. All qualifying chronic/severe diagnoses from claims/encounters in the model year. Provider documentation abstracted to ICD codes; POA, laterality, encounter type, etc. Outpatient CPT/HCPCS codes, status indicators, units; sometimes device edits, composite logic.
Governing body CMS (Medicare IPPS; MS-DRGs). CMS (HCPCS Level II) and AMA (CPT = HCPCS Level I). CMS (MA/ACA risk models; state variations for Medicaid). WHO (ICD), with U.S. maintenance by NCHS (ICD-10-CM) and CMS (ICD-10-PCS). CMS (OPPS/APC program).
DRG
  • Creation (claim/part): Assigned by grouper to institutional inpatient (UB-04/837I) claims — Type of Bill 11x, paid under Medicare Part A (IPPS). Medicaid IP claims in TAF also use UB-04 (state policies vary). Inputs: principal DX, procedures, CC/MCC, age/sex, discharge status.
  • Input to bundling/VBC: Payment unit for IPPS (Part A); anchors episodes in DRG-based cost measures and readmissions programs (HRRP); state Medicaid programs may use DRG-based IP payment.
  • Used with other data: Wage index, case-mix, outlier thresholds, discharge/transfer status, transfer rules.
HCPCS (CPT Level I / Level II)
  • Creation (claim/part): Reported on professional (CMS-1500/837P) and hospital outpatient (UB-04/837I, TOB 13x) claims in Medicare Part B; also used in MA (C) encounters and Medicaid (TAF). Level I maintained by AMA (CPT); Level II by CMS.
  • Input to bundling/VBC: Drives OPPS/APC grouping (Part B), MPFS pricing (B), and episode/cost measures in value-based programs; similar logic in MA/Medicaid payment edits.
  • Used with other data: Status indicators (OPPS B), units, modifiers (25/59/JW/JZ), revenue codes, NDC for drugs (especially Part D contexts).
HCC — CMS Risk Adjustment
  • Creation (claim/part): Derived annually from ICD-10-CM diagnoses on institutional (UB-04/837I: A/B) and professional (1500/837P: B) claims across settings; computes RAF for Medicare Advantage (Part C); some Medicaid programs adopt HCC-like risk models.
  • Input to bundling/VBC: Adjusts MA (C) capitation and ACO benchmarks; used for risk stratification and performance attribution.
  • Used with other data: Demographics (age/sex/dual/disability), model interactions, encounter completeness rules to produce final RAF.
APC
  • Creation (claim/part): Assigned to hospital outpatient (UB-04/837I) claims — TOB 13x, paid under Medicare Part B (OPPS). State Medicaid programs may use OPPS-like rules. Built from CPT/HCPCS + status indicators (includes packaging and C-APC logic).
  • Input to bundling/VBC: Payment unit for OPPS (B); packaging creates de facto ambulatory bundles; used in outpatient episode/cost measures in VBP contexts.
  • Used with other data: OPPS conversion factor, wage index, multiple-procedure discounting, device/drug pass-through status, required modifiers.
Claim Forms & Type of Bill Codes
UB-04 / Form CMS-1450 → 837I Institutional claim form/transaction. Used for Medicare A/B facility billing to MACs and by many Medicaid State Agencies for institutional claims. Setting indicator: Type of Bill (TOB).
CMS-1500 → 837P Professional (non-institutional) claim form/transaction. Used for Medicare Part B physician/supplier claims and Medicaid professional billing. Stewarded by NUCC. Setting indicator: Place of Service (POS).
TOB 11x Hospital Inpatient (e.g., 111 = admit-through-discharge). Claims paid under Medicare Part A — IPPS, DRG-based payment. Medicaid institutional IP claims in TAF also carry TOB.
TOB 13x Hospital Outpatient (e.g., 131 = outpatient claim). Claims paid under Medicare Part B — OPPS; APC-based payment applies.
Place of Service (POS) 2-digit code on professional claims (CMS-1500/837P) identifying the care setting. Required for Medicare Part B; widely used by Medicaid and other payers.

Elements by Analytic Domain
Cost (payment/pricing)
  • Institutional (UB-04/837I): claim-level CLM_PMT_AMT; sum of revenue-center payment/charge lines in Medicare A (IP) and B (OP). Medicaid TAF has analogous paid/allowed fields.
  • Professional (CMS-1500/837P): allowed/paid amounts under Medicare Part B (Carrier/DME), line-level and claim-level rollups; similar constructs in Medicaid TAF non-institutional.
  • Pharmacy (Part D PDE): gross drug cost, plan paid, patient pay, LIS, TrOOP contributors under Medicare Part D; Medicaid pharmacy has parallel elements in TAF.
  • Plan context: Part C/D plan characteristics (premiums/service area) for benchmarking; state/plan constructs in Medicaid.
Utilization (counts/intensity)
  • Units: HCPCS lines, revenue-center units (OP), E/M visits, admissions/stays (IP/SNF via MedPAR roll-ups under Medicare A), days supply (PDE Part D).
  • Dates: CLM_FROM/THRU, ADMSN/DSCHRG for LOS, readmissions, episode triggers — primarily Medicare A/B/C; fill date for D; analogous in Medicaid TAF.
  • Setting indicators: TOB 11x (IP/Part A), 13x (OP/Part B) for site-of-service splits; POS for B/C professional/encounter claims; Medicaid uses TOB/POS analogs in TAF.
Disease Burden (clinical severity/risk)
  • Diagnoses: ICD-10-CM across A/B/C (and Medicaid TAF) feed HCC risk models used for MA (C) and sometimes Medicaid.
  • Procedures: add clinical complexity — ICD-10-PCS in IP Part A, HCPCS/CPT in B/C.
  • Part D (D): contributes medication exposure and adherence metrics for chronic disease burden analysis.
Mini-Glossary
BENE_ID Encrypted CCW Beneficiary ID. Stable, linkable key across CCW files for Medicare FFS (A/B), MA encounters (C), Part D (D), and CCW-linked Medicaid (TAF). Use: link claims/encounters/PDEs to enrollment and each other; build denominators.
CLM_ID Claim header identifier. Revenue-center/line segments appear beneath the header. Exists in Medicare FFS (A/B), MA encounters (C), and analogous constructs in Medicaid TAF (institutional/professional).
CLM_FROM_DT / CLM_THRU_DT "Statement covers from/thru" dates. Present in Medicare FFS (A/B) and MA encounters (C). Part D uses fill date variables; TAF carries service dates for Medicaid.
CLM_PMT_AMT Claim-level Medicare payment amount on institutional (UB-04/837I) claims. Paired with revenue-center line amounts for granular cost analysis.
MedPAR Medicare Part A stay-level roll-up from inpatient hospital and SNF claims. Convenient for admissions/LOS analytics; not line-level.
RAF (Risk Adjustment Factor) The numeric risk score produced by the HCC model. Combines HCC scores and demographic factors (age/sex/dual/disability). Applied to MA capitation benchmarks and ACO financial benchmarks.
NDC (National Drug Code) 11-digit drug identifier on Part D Prescription Drug Events (PDEs) and Medicaid pharmacy claims. Identifies the specific drug, strength, and package size.
TAF (T-MSIS Analytic Files) Research-optimized Medicaid claims/encounters (institutional and non-institutional), eligibility, and provider files derived from T-MSIS. Access via ResDAC/CMS.
NPI (National Provider Identifier) 10-digit identifier for individual providers and organizations. Present in Medicare A/B/C/D contexts; Medicaid TAF has parallel provider keys. Use to link claims to POS file, HCRIS, and MIPS performance data.
